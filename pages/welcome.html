refresh  →  am broadcast -n com.dsh.rearvibe/.VibeReceiver -a dsh.vibe.REFRESH
switch  →  -a dsh.vibe.NEXT  |  PREV  |  LOAD --es page x.html
safe area → put content inside .safe (left: var(--cam) = 296px)
